## Ecological Mechanics
Soil organic carbon (SOC) levels under 0.5% severely starve heterotrophic soil microorganisms of labile carbon. When soil pH is slightly alkaline (pH 7.5 - 8.2), phosphorus availability is additionally chemically bound to calcium. Re-introducing organic matter via humified compost, multi-species green manures, and retaining crop stubble restores microbial decomposition pathways, elevates microbial biomass carbon by 35-50%, and improves soil aggregate stability. This structural enhancement doubles water infiltration rates, buffering crops and soil biotas against low rainfall and prolonged dry spells.
