Title: Agroecological Perimeter Buffer Strips for Pollinator Conservation
Authors: IPBES Pollination Working Group
Organization: IPBES
Year: 2021
Topic: biodiversity
URL: https://www.ipbes.net/pollinators
Region: Temperate and Subtropical Agricultural Zones
Metrics: pollinator_abundance, habitat_diversity, pesticide_pressure

Content:
Intensive agricultural monocultures deplete floral diversity, exposing insect pollinators to chronic nutritional stress and insecticide toxicity. Establishing native flowering vegetative buffers (5 to 10 meters wide) on field boundaries creates crucial ecological refuges. Diverse native flora flowering asynchronously ensures season-long nectar and pollen availability. This simple intervention raises wild solitary bee species richness by 45-65% and reduces pesticide drift into surrounding native ecosystems by 60%.
